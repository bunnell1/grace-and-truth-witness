# Grace & Truth Witness

A preparation aid for believers in Christ (reformed Protestants) to share their faith in ordinary conversations.

This is a working prototype built in the same style as [Grace & Truth Counsel](https://graceandtruthcounsel.com/). It offers a searchable database of situations and things people say, biblical principles, sample response directions, Scripture, prayer guidance, and printable tools.

## What it is for
Helping a believer show up prepared to speak the truth in love: clear gospel, listening, biblical principles, and dependence on the Spirit. Search what someone said or describe the situation. Save and print conversation notes or a simple gospel outline.

## What it is not
It is not a script, a method that guarantees conversion, or a substitute for prayer, the local church, or the work of the Holy Spirit. Sample responses are direction, not lines to recite. Entered notes stay on your device.

## Tech
- GitHub Pages (static)
- Optional Supabase for account / favorites
- ESV.org for Scripture lookups

## Convictions
- The gospel is the power of God for salvation (Romans 1:16).
- The Bible is sufficient. We do not need secular techniques.
- Salvation is of the Lord. We sow; God gives the increase.
- Speak the truth in love. Grace and truth together.
- The local church is the place of ongoing care and follow-up.

Built for ordinary believers who want to be ready.
