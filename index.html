About

Grace & Truth Witness is a preparation aid for believers who want to share Christ faithfully in ordinary conversations. It is meant to be used with Scripture open, alongside prayer and the local church.

Statement of Convictions

  • The gospel is the power of God for salvation (Romans 1:16).
  • The Bible is sufficient. We do not need secular techniques to share Christ.
  • Salvation is of the Lord. We sow the seed; the Spirit gives the increase.
  • Speak the truth in love. Grace and truth together honor Christ.
  • The local church is the place of ongoing care and follow-up.

What this site is for

Helping a believer show up prepared: applicable Scripture, biblical principles, sample response directions, and care for the speaker’s own heart.

What this site is not

It is not a script, a guarantee of conversion, or a substitute for the Spirit, prayer, or the local church. Sample responses are direction, not lines to recite. Entered data stays on your device.

Add to Home Screen

You can add Grace & Truth Witness to your home screen so it opens like an app.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Add or Install.
